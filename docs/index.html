What it does with "I want to put the rent up in January"

You type:

"Monthly tenancy, rent £950 due on the 20th. Started 20 March 2024, last increase 20 January 2026. I'd like £1,000 from January. What do I need to do?"

↓ and get back

The dates

2 months' notice from service · 52 weeks after the tenancy began (met) · 52 weeks after 20 January 2026 = 19 January 2027 · must start on a rent day (the 20th).

Earliest start: Wednesday 20 January 2027.

Serve Form 4A by hand by Friday 20 November 2026, or post it a few working days earlier.

Increase: £50 a month (5.3%), £600 a year. Check 3–5 similar homes nearby on the portals before you settle on the figure.

For you

  1. Form 4A from gov.uk, unchanged: 4.1 £950, 4.5 £1,000 (per month), 4.2 20/03/2024, 4.3 20/01/2026.
  2. A short message to talk it through with your tenant first.
  3. A friendly covering letter to go with the form.
  4. Diary: serve by 20 Nov, keep proof, new rent 20 Jan 2027, next possible rise 20 January 2028.

Every date is worked out from what you typed. Comparable rents come from you, never made up.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The three skills are free and open source (MIT). You need ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude; on a free plan, use the "Any chat app" route above. Landlord Skills Pro adds six more (new tenancies, arrears, deposits, a compliance calendar, the landlord database and year-end records) for £19, paid once.

Does it work in Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland?

The tenancy rules are written for private tenancies in England under the Renters' Rights Act 2025, in force from 1 May 2026. Wales (Renting Homes), Scotland and Northern Ireland have different laws, and the skills say so instead of applying England's rules.

Can I still use a rent review clause?

No. Since 1 May 2026 the rent can only go up with a Section 13 notice on Form 4A, at most once a year, with at least 2 months' notice, starting on a rent day. You need the form even if the tenant agrees. Source: gov.uk landlord guide.

How long do I have to answer a tenant's pet request?

28 days, in writing. You can ask for more information within those 28 days; once you get it, you have the rest of the 28 days or 7 more days, whichever is later. A refusal needs a reason, and assistance animals shouldn't be refused.

How fast do I have to fix a repair?

The law says "within a reasonable time" of being told, and that gets shorter as the risk grows. The repair-requests skill uses good-practice targets: make emergencies safe within 24 hours, urgent jobs in 3–5 days, routine ones in 2–4 weeks. A faulty smoke or CO alarm must be repaired or replaced as soon as reasonably practicable.

Is it legal advice?

No. The skills draft and you decide. They follow gov.uk guidance and tell you what to check, but they don't replace a solicitor or a letting agent, and disputes or possession need proper advice.
